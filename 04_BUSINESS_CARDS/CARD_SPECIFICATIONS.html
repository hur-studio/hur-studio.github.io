01. Executive Business Card (3.5" × 2.0" / 89mm × 51mm)

Dimensions & Bleed

  • Trim Size: 3.5 in × 2.0 in (88.9 mm × 50.8 mm)
  • Bleed Size: 3.75 in × 2.25 in (95.25 mm × 57.15 mm — 0.125 in bleed on all 4 sides)
  • Safe Zone: 3.25 in × 1.75 in (Keep all critical text and icons within this boundary)

Paper Stock & Finishing Specifications

  • Stock: 450 gsm (32 pt) Ultra-Heavyweight Silk Matte Cover Stock
  • Finish: Double-sided Soft-Touch Velvet Lamination (subtle tactile warmth, anti-fingerprint)
  • Foil Stamping: Hot-Stamped Satin Antique Gold Foil on the primary wordmark and motto (READ. REFLECT. RETURN.)
  • Spot Finish: Blind Debossing on the geometric archway emblem (subtle indented relief)
  • Edges: Optional Gilded Antique Gold Foil Edge-Gilding for executive founder sets

Typography & Palette

  • Primary Display: Cinzel (Semibold 10pt / Kerning +180)
  • Body & Metadata: Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans (Regular & Light 7.5pt / Kerning +40)
  • Scripture Quote: Cormorant Garamond (Italic 9pt)
  • Color Palette:
    • Obsidian Navy: #0a0d13 (Rich Dark Surface)
    • Metallic Antique Gold: #d4af59 (Foil Stamp & Accents)
    • Muted Sand White: #e2e8f0 (High-Contrast Text)

02. Pocket Contemplation Anchor Card

Concept & Purpose

A tactile reminder card distributed to partners, event attendees, and major patrons. It serves as a pocket sanctuary during moments of worldly stress or decision-making.

Front Design: Visual motif of mountain stream rippling over pebbles in dawn light. Scripture: "My Lord, expand for me my chest, and ease for me my task." (Surah Ta-Ha 20:25–26). Action: "Take three deep breaths. Disconnect from worldly panic. Return to your Creator."

Back Design: Discrete QR code leading directly to the offline audio reflection stream and portfolio portal.
