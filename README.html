04. Production & Printing Guide

Printing the Trifold Brochure

  • Paper Stock: 150–170 gsm silk/satin coated text stock (prevents show-through while allowing crisp folding).
  • Printer Settings: Standard US Letter (8.5" × 11") or A4, Landscape orientation, Scale: 100% (Do not fit/shrink).
  • Duplexing: Set printer to Two-Sided (Flip on Short Edge).
  • Folding: Fold Panel 5 inward first from the left, then fold Panel 1 (Front Cover) over top from the right.

Printing the Business Cards

  • Paper Stock: 400–450 gsm heavyweight silk card stock.
  • Finishing: Soft-touch velvet matte lamination with satin antique gold hot-foil stamping on card borders and crests.
  • Printer Settings: Standard US Letter (8.5" × 11") Portrait, Two-Sided (Flip on Long Edge).
  • Trimming: Follow the corner crop tick marks with a rotary guillotine cutter to achieve exact 3.5" × 2.0" dimensions.
