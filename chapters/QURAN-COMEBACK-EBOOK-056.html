CHAPTER 56: SURAH AL-WAQI'AH (سُورَةُ الواقعة)

Classification: Makki

Order in Mushaf: 56

Total Verses: 96

Famous Names: Surat al-Waqi'ah, Surat al-Ghina (The Chapter of Wealth)

1. Why This Surah Matters

Surah Al-Waqi'ah is the definitive cinematic roadmap of humanity's ultimate sorting. It takes its name from Al-Waqi'ah—the Inevitable, Cataclysmic Event of the Day of Judgment, when all worldly social hierarchies are obliterated, performing its terrifying cosmic function: Khafidatun Rafi'ah ('Abasing [some] and exalting [others]'). It divides all of human history into three distinct spiritual tiers: The People of the Left (Ashab ash-Shimal - the condemned), the People of the Right (Ashab al-Yamin - the ordinary saved), and the elite vanguard: As-Sabiqun as-Sabiqun (Those Foremost in Faith, who are the foremost in proximity to Allah). It is cherished as the spiritual shield against poverty, traditionally recited every night.

2. Surah Context

Revealed in Makkah to confront secular skeptics who viewed death as the absolute end. Surah Al-Waqi'ah proved resurrection through the four everyday miracles humans take for granted: human semen, agricultural seeds, freshwater rain, and fire.

3. Central Theme

The Inevitable Sorting of Humanity into Three Tiers, the Abolition of Worldly Hierarchies (Khafidah Rafi'ah), and the Four Empirical Proofs of Resurrection.

4. Key Passages

Abasing and Exalting (56:1–3)

$$\text{إِذَا وَقَعَتِ الْوَاقِعَةُ ۝ لَيْسَ لِوَقْعَتِهَا كَاذِبَةٌ ۝ خَافِضَةٌ رَّافِعَةٌ}$$

"When the Inevitable Event occurs, There is no, at its occurrence, denial. It will bring down [some] and raise up [others]."

The Three Spiritual Tiers (56:7–10)

$$\text{وَكُنتُمْ أَزْوَاجًا ثَلَاثَةً ۝ فَأَصْحَابُ الْمَيْمَنَةِ مَا أَصْحَابُ الْمَيْمَنَةِ ۝ وَأَصْحَابُ الْمَشْأَمَةِ مَا أَصْحَابُ الْمَشْأَمَةِ ۝ وَالسَّابِقُونَ السَّابِقُونَ ۝ أُولَٰئِكَ الْمُقَرَّبُونَ}$$

"And you become [three] kinds: Then the companions of the right—what are the companions of the right? And the companions of the left—what are the companions of the left? And the forerunners, the forerunners—Those are the ones brought near [to Allah]."

The Four Signs: Seed and Water (56:63–69)

$$\text{أَفَرَأَيْتُم مَّا تَحْرُثُونَ ۝ أَأَنتُمْ تَزْرَعُونَهُ أَمْ نَحْنُ الزَّارِعُونَ... أَفَرَأَيْتُمُ الْمَاءَ الَّذِي تَشْرَبُونَ ۝ أَأَنتُمْ أَنزَلْتُمُوهُ مِنَ الْمُزْنِ أَمْ نَحْنُ الْمُنزِلُونَ}$$

"Have you seen that which you sow? Is it you who makes it grow, or are We the grower?... Have you seen the water that you drink? Is it you who brought it down from the clouds, or are We the sender?"

5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)

  • 56:77–80: 'Indeed, it is a noble Quran In a Register well-protected; None touch it except the purified. [It is] a revelation from the Lord of the worlds.'
  • 56:83–85: 'Then why, when the soul at death reaches the throat, And you are at that time looking on—And Our angels are nearer to him than you, but you do not see...'
  • 56:96: 'So exalt the name of your Lord, the Most Great.'

6. What Classical Tafsir Explains

  • What Does 'Khafidatun Rafi'ah' Mean? (Verse 3): Umar ibn al-Khattab and Imam Ibn Kathir explain: 'It abases the arrogant who held immense status, wealth, and power in this world, dropping them into the lowest depths of Hell; and it raises up the humble, impoverished believers who were ignored or mocked on earth, elevating them to the highest stations of Jannah.' Worldly resumes are discarded; only souls are weighed.
  • Who Are 'As-Sabiqun'? (Verse 10): Ibn Sirin noted: 'They are those who pray toward both Qiblahs, or those who hasten first to the mosques, or those who hasten to every good deed.' The rule is: Whoever is a Sabiq (foremost) to good deeds in this world will be a Sabiq to Jannah and divine nearness (Al-Muqarrabun) in the Hereafter.

7. Important Supporting Hadiths

1. The Protection from Poverty:

Shu'ab al-Iman by Al-Bayhaqi (Hasan lighayrihi):

"Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (RA) reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: 'Whoever recites Surah Al-Waqi'ah every night will never be afflicted by poverty.' When Ibn Mas'ud was on his deathbed and offered money by Uthman ibn Affan, he refused, saying: 'I have commanded my daughters to recite Surah Al-Waqi'ah every night.'"

2. Exalting the Great Lord:

Sunan Abi Dawud (Hadith 869), Sahih:

"When the verse Fa-sabbih bi-ismi Rabbikal-'Adhim (56:96) was revealed, the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'Place it in your bowing (Ruku').' Hence, the Sunnah of saying Subhana Rabbiyal-'Adhim was established."

8. Key Concepts

1. Al-Waqi'ah (The Inevitable Cataclysm): The day of total reckoning that brooks no denial and sorts all humanity irrevocably.

2. Khafidatun Rafi'ah (The Great Reversal): The cosmic inversion of worldly status: humbling the arrogant and elevating the righteous.

3. As-Sabiqun (The Foremost Vanguard): The highest spiritual elite who raced to good deeds on earth, stationed in supreme nearness to God.

4. Maknun / Mutahharun (The Purified Book): The pristine celestial tablet and Mushaf touched only by purified angels and clean hands.

9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)

  • The Great Status Inversion (56:3): Think about how modern culture ranks humans: by Forbes net worth, Instagram followers, executive titles, and luxury zip codes. On Judgment Day, Surah Al-Waqi'ah executes a total reversal: Khafidatun Rafi'ah. A CEO who neglected Allah will be abased into the dust, while a quiet janitor who woke up for Tahajjud will be raised above the clouds in the ranks of As-Sabiqun. Live for the ranking that never drops.
  • Are You Really the Farmer? (56:63–64): Look at the question: A-antum tazra'unahu am Nahnu az-zari'un? You buy the seed and drop it into dirt. But who programmed the genetic code inside that seed? Who sends the rain? Who commands the soil chemistry to split the seed, push a green shoot upward against gravity, and yield golden wheat? You are not the producer; you are merely a spectator of divine miracles. Be humble.

10. What This Means For Me

  • I make it a daily practice to recite Surah Al-Waqi'ah at night, seeking both spiritual richness and divine protection.
  • I strive to be among As-Sabiqun (the foremost) by hastening to voluntary good deeds without hesitating (56:10).
  • Whenever I bow in prayer, I recite Subhana Rabbiyal-'Adhim with deep awe, fulfilling the command of verse 56:96.

11. One Practical Action

Recite Surah Al-Waqi'ah Tonight Before Sleeping:

Revive the sunnah of Ibn Mas'ud: Read Surah Al-Waqi'ah tonight with translation, contemplating the three categories of souls, and ask Allah to make you among As-Sabiqun.

12. Reflection Questions

1. Which of the three groups will I belong to when the Inevitable Event occurs (56:7–10)?

2. Am I striving to be among As-Sabiqun (the foremost in good deeds) or am I content with spiritual mediocrity?

3. How does reflecting on Khafidatun Rafi'ah (56:3) change how I evaluate worldly prestige and power?

13. Closing Supplication

$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْنَا مِنَ السَّابِقِينَ السَّابِقِينَ ، أُولَٰئِكَ الْمُقَرَّبُونَ فِي جَنَّاتِ النَّعِيمِ ، اللَّهُمَّ ارْفَعْنَا وَلَا تَخْفِضْنَا ، وَسَبِّحْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ الْعَظِيمِ}$$

"O Allah, make us among the forerunners, the forerunners—those brought near in the Gardens of Bliss. O Allah, elevate us and do not abase us. Glorified be the name of our Lord, the Most Great. Amin."
