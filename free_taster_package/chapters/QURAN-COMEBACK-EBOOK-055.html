CHAPTER 55: SURAH AR-RAHMAN (سُورَةُ الرحمن)

Classification: Madani

Order in Mushaf: 55

Total Verses: 78

Famous Names: Surat ar-Rahman, 'Arus al-Qur'an (The Bride of the Qur'an)

1. Why This Surah Matters

Surah Ar-Rahman is celebrated across the Muslim world as 'Arus al-Qur'an (The Bride of the Qur'an) because of its sublime poetic beauty, cosmic harmony, and dazzling rhythm. It is structured around the iconic divine question addressed to humans and jinn, repeated like a celestial heartbeat thirty-one times: 'So which of the favors of your Lord will you deny?' (Fabi-ayyi ala'i Rabbikuma tukadhdhiban). It takes the soul on a panoramic tour of existence: teaching the Qur'an before physical creation, the sun and moon calculated in precise orbits, the earth laid down for living creatures, the two roaring seas meeting without transgression, and culminates in the ultimate reality of our mortality: 'Everyone upon the earth will perish, and there will remain the Face of your Lord, Owner of Majesty and Honor' (55:26–27).

2. Surah Context

Revealed to both worlds—mankind and jinn (Ath-Thaqalayn)—celebrating the boundless gifts of the Creator. When the Prophet ﷺ recited it to the jinn, they were more appreciative than humans, responding to every repetition of Fabi-ayyi ala'i Rabbikuma tukadhdhiban by saying: 'Not one of Your blessings, our Lord, do we deny; to You belongs all praise!'

3. Central Theme

The Countless Manifestations of Divine Mercy (Ar-Rahmah), the Cosmic Balance (Al-Mizan), the Mortality of Creation, and the Splendors of Jannah.

4. Key Passages

The Curriculum of Creation (55:1–4)

$$\text{الرَّحْمَٰنُ ۝ عَلَّمَ الْقُرْآنَ ۝ خَلَقَ الْإِنسَانَ ۝ عَلَّمَهُ الْبَيَانَ}$$

"The Most Merciful, Taught the Quran, Created man, [And] taught him eloquent speech."

The Golden Rule of Justice and Balance (55:7–9)

$$\text{وَالسَّمَاءَ رَفَعَهَا وَوَضَعَ الْمِيزَانَ ۝ أَلَّا تَطْغَوْا فِي الْمِيزَانِ ۝ وَأَقِيمُوا الْوَزْنَ بِالْقِسْطِ وَلَا تُخْسِرُوا الْمِيزَانَ}$$

"And the heaven He raised and imposed the balance, That you not transgress within the balance. And establish weight in justice and do not make deficient the balance."

The Transience of All Things (55:26–27)

$$\text{كُلُّ مَنْ عَلَيْهَا فَانٍ ۝ وَيَبْقَىٰ وَجْهُ رَبِّكَ ذُو الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ}$$

"Everyone upon the earth will perish, And there will remain the Face of your Lord, Owner of Majesty and Honor."

The Law of Equal Reward (55:60)

$$\text{هَلْ جَزَاءُ الْإِحْسَانِ إِلَّا الْإِحْسَانُ}$$

"Is the reward for excellence [in good] anything other than excellence?"

5. Translation Highlights (The Clear Quran)

  • 55:19–20: 'He released the two seas, meeting [side by side]; Between them is a barrier [so] neither of them transgresses.'
  • 55:29: 'Whoever is within the heavens and earth asks Him; every day He is bringing about a matter.'
  • 55:78: 'Blessed is the name of your Lord, Owner of Majesty and Honor.'

6. What Classical Tafsir Explains

  • Why Does Teaching the Qur'an Precede Human Creation? (Verses 1–3): Imam Ibn Kathir and classical mufassirun point out the astonishing sequence: Allah says He taught the Qur'an before mentioning that He created man! Why? Because biological life without divine revelation has no true human value. Revelation is the purpose and instruction manual of human existence; without it, man is merely an animal.
  • Every Day He is Upon an Affair (Verse 29): Ibn Abbas explained Kulla yawmin Huwa fi sha'n: 'Of His daily affairs is that He forgives sins, relieves distress, elevates a humble person, debases an arrogant tyrant, cures the sick, answers the caller, and gives to the asker.' Allah is not a distant, inactive deity; He actively governs the cosmos every millisecond.

7. Important Supporting Hadiths

1. The Appreciation of the Jinn:

Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 3291), Hasan:

"Jabir (RA) narrated: 'The Prophet ﷺ recited Surah Ar-Rahman to his companions, and they remained silent. He said: I recited it to the jinn on the night of the jinn, and they had a better response than you! Whenever I recited "Fabi-ayyi ala'i Rabbikuma tukadhdhiban", they said: "La bi-shay'in min ni'amika Rabbana nukadhdhibu, falakal-hamd" (Not one of Your favors, our Lord, do we deny; to You belongs all praise!).'"

2. The Exclusivity of Ihsan:

Sunan al-Bayhaqi in Shu'ab al-Iman (Hasan):

"Regarding verse 55:60 (Hal jaza'u al-ihsani illal-ihsan), the Prophet ﷺ said: 'Allah says: Is the reward of someone upon whom I bestowed monotheism anything other than Paradise?'"

8. Key Concepts

1. 'Arus al-Qur'an (The Bride of the Qur'an): The title signifying the peak aesthetic and rhetorical splendor of Surah Ar-Rahman.

2. Al-Mizan (The Cosmic Balance): The universal principle of equilibrium, ecological balance, and ethical justice governing the cosmos.

3. Kullu Man 'Alayha Fan (Universal Mortality): The sobering certainty that all empires, creatures, and planets will dissolve, leaving only Allah.

4. Jaza'u al-Ihsan (The Echo of Excellence): The ironclad divine promise that moral excellence (Ihsan) is always met with divine benevolence.

9. Tadabbur (Guided Contemplation)

  • Can You Deny a Single Blessing? (55:13): When the verse asks: Fabi-ayyi ala'i Rabbikuma tukadhdhiban, look at your life. The air you breathe, the retinas interpreting these words, the liver filtering your toxins, the loved ones who smile at you, the glass of cool water—all of it is an unearned gift from Ar-Rahman. How can anyone complain about life when we are drowning in an ocean of favors? Answer with the jinn: Falakal-Hamd (All praise is Yours).
  • Every Day He is Bringing About an Affair (55:29): If you are stuck in a dead-end, facing an illness, or praying for a door to open, remember: Kulla yawmin Huwa fi sha'n. Today, Allah is turning someone's poverty into wealth, someone's grief into laughter, and someone's failure into victory. Never think your situation is static; Allah is actively rewriting human stories every second.

10. What This Means For Me

  • I respond to every blessing in my life with the words of the jinn: 'Not a single one of Your favors, our Lord, do we deny; to You belongs all praise!'
  • I uphold justice and honesty in all my transactions, refusing to cheat or make the balance deficient (55:9).
  • I practice excellence (Ihsan) in my work, worship, and relationships, confident that excellence only brings divine excellence (55:60).

11. One Practical Action

Conduct a '31-Blessings' Gratitude Inventory:

Take a blank piece of paper today and write down 31 specific blessings in your life that you rarely thank Allah for (e.g., functioning lungs, peaceful sleep, running water, forgiveness of sins). Conclude by saying Alhamdulillah Rabbil-'Alamin.

12. Reflection Questions

1. Which of Allah's countless blessings am I currently taking for granted (55:13)?

2. How does realizing that 'everyone upon earth will perish' (55:26) free me from petty worldly grudges?

3. Am I giving Ihsan (excellence) in my worship and daily work, expecting Allah's Ihsan in return (55:60)?

13. Closing Supplication

$$\text{اللَّهُمَّ يَا رَحْمَٰنُ يَا رَحِيمُ ، لَا بِشَيْءٍ مِنْ نِعَمِكَ رَبَّنَا نُكَذِّبُ فَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ ، اللَّهُمَّ يَا مَنْ كُلَّ يَوْمٍ هُوَ فِي شَأْنٍ فَرِّجْ هُمُومَنَا ، وَاجْعَلْ جَزَاءَنَا الْإِحْسَانَ فِي جَنَّاتِ النَّعِيمِ}$$

"O Allah, O Entirely Merciful, O Especially Merciful: Not one of Your favors, our Lord, do we deny; to You belongs all praise. O Allah, O One who every day is bringing about an affair, relieve our anxieties, and make our reward excellence in the Gardens of Bliss. Amin."
