The Tripartite Partition of Humanity (56:7–56)

Classical Sunni tafsir establishes that humanity on Judgment Day is irrevocably separated into three distinct ranks rather than a simple binary: (1) As-Sabiqun (The Foremost) who outpaced all others in sincere faith and sacrificial excellence; (2) As-hab al-Yameen (Companions of the Right) who maintained righteousness and received their records in honor; and (3) As-hab ash-Shimal (Companions of the Left) who indulged in worldly luxury, denied resurrection, and broke covenants.

Imam Ibn Kathir relates that the Sahabah wept upon hearing that the Foremost were numerous from early nations but fewer from later ones, until Allah comforted them by revealing that great multitudes of both early and latter believers will enter the Gardens of the Right Hand.

Authorities: Musnad Ahmad • Ibn Abi Hatim • Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/3) • Tafsir at-Tabari (23/98)

The Four Empirical Proofs of Sovereign Resurrection (56:57–74)

Surah Al-Waqi'ah deploys a rigorous quadripartite dialectic challenging human autonomy across everyday survival necessities: (1) Human procreation: Did you create the seminal drop, or is Allah the sole author of biological form? (2) Agrarian sustenance: Do you cause the seed in the earth to sprout, or does Allah govern germination?

(3) Hydrological reliance: Did you distill the sweet rainclouds from the atmosphere, or does Allah spare you from bitter brine? (4) Energetic mastery: Did you produce the combustible wood that yields fire, or did Allah embed energy storage in living trees? Imam ar-Razi highlights that these four foundational proofs demonstrate humanity's total ontological dependence.

Authorities: Tafsir al-Qurtubi (17/215) • Mafatih al-Ghayb (Razi 29/172) • Tafsir al-Baghawi

The Monumental Oath by Celestial Settings (56:75–80)

In Ayah 75-77, Allah swears an oath of cosmic majesty by 'Mawaqi' an-Nujoom' (the positions, descents, and gravitational orbits of celestial bodies), declaring: 'And indeed, it is a mighty oath if you only knew.'

Classical commentators note that this astronomical oath serves as divine validation for the nobility, purity, and transcendence of the Quran. Encased in a 'Kitab Maknoon' (Well-Guarded Book) upon the Preserved Tablet, it is protected from all satanic corruption and touched only by purified angelic messengers. It repudiates any Meccan claim that revelation was a poetic hallucination.

Authorities: Sahih al-Bukhari • Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/19) • Tafsir at-Tabari (23/140)

The Agony of Death & The Sovereign Distance (56:83–96)

The climax of Surah Al-Waqi'ah confronts human pride with the universal scene of the deathbed. When the soul rattles in the throat (al-hulqoom), the most powerful monarchs, skilled physicians, and loving relatives stand frozen in absolute impotence.

At that instant, Allah declares: 'And We are nearer to him than you, but you do not perceive.' If mankind truly possesses sovereign independence and is exempt from divine judgment, why cannot they pull back the departing soul into the chest? This piercing rhetorical inquiry exposes existential fragility and seals the truth of the inevitable return.

Authorities: Tafsir Ibn Kathir (8/24) • Tafsir al-Qurtubi (17/230) • Al-Bahr al-Muheet
